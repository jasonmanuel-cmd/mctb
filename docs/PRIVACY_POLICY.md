# Privacy Policy

**Missed Call Reply**

_Last updated: 2026-10-19_

## The short version

The app does not collect your data, does not send your data anywhere, and has no
account, no analytics and no advertising. Phone numbers it sees stay on your phone and
are deleted automatically within 30 minutes.

## What the app does

Missed Call Reply watches for incoming phone calls you do not answer, and prepares a
text message you can send with one tap. To do that it uses two Android capabilities.

### Call screening

Android's call screening system tells the app the phone number of an incoming call
before the phone rings. The app records that number in local storage on your device so
that, if the call then rings out unanswered, it knows who to prepare a reply for.

The number is held for **30 minutes at most**. It is overwritten by the next incoming
call, and every expired entry is deleted on each write. It is never uploaded, never
shared, and never used for anything other than deciding whether you have already been
prompted about this caller recently.

### Phone state

The app is told when a call starts ringing, is answered, or ends. It uses this only to
work out whether a call went unanswered. The app does **not** read your call log.

## What the app does not do

- Does not read your call history or call log.
- Does not read, send, receive or store your text messages. Replies are handed to your
  own messaging app; the app never sends a message itself.
- Does not read your contacts.
- Does not record or transmit calls.
- Does not contain analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking SDKs.
- Does not require an account, an email address or any personal information.
- Does not transmit any data to any server, including the developer's.

## Permissions

| Permission | Purpose | Revocable |
|---|---|---|
| Call screening role | See the number of an incoming call | Yes — Android Settings → Apps → Special app access |
| Phone state | Detect when a call goes unanswered | Yes — Android Settings |
| Notifications | Show you the reply prompt | Yes — Android Settings |

All three are revocable at any time in Android's settings, and the app stops working if
you withdraw them.

## Purchases

The optional unlimited upgrade is processed entirely by Google Play. The app receives
only a confirmation that the purchase succeeded. Google handles all payment details;
the app never sees your card, billing address or any other payment information.

## Data storage and deletion

Everything the app stores lives in its own private storage area on your device, which
Android sandboxes away from other apps. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it. You can
also clear it at any time via Android Settings → Apps → Missed Call Reply → Storage →
Clear data.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

If this policy changes, the updated version will ship with the app update and the
